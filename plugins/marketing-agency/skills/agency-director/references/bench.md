# The agency bench

Use role names, not celebrity identities. Expert lenses contain sourced observations
and explicitly authored agency applications. They do not reconstruct private thinking.

| Role | Read | Main methods | Returns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Director | [director](agents/director.md) | brief and routing | decision, integrated result |
| Product & launch strategist | [launch](agents/launch-strategist.md) | product-marketing, launch | positioning, launch sequence |
| Research & culture strategist | [research](agents/research-strategist.md) | customer-research, content-strategy | evidence, territories |
| Creative director & copywriter | [creative](agents/creative-director.md) | copywriting, content-strategy | concept, script, exact copy |
| Art director & visual designer | [art](agents/art-director.md) | visual-production | visual system, assets, layouts |
| Motion director & editor | [motion](agents/motion-director.md) | reference-restage, installed renderer | timed storyboard, film |
| Producer & quality reviewer | [review](agents/quality-producer.md) | content-review | readiness, evidence, repair list |
| Distribution & learning strategist | [distribution](agents/distribution-strategist.md) | analytics, launch | channel plan, results, next test |

When feasible, the quality reviewer should not be the creator of the final piece.
If one agent performs both, explicitly label this a self-review. Specialists can
use other skills when needed; these assignments are starting points, not tool prisons.
