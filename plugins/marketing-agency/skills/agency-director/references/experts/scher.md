# Paula Scher: typography and identity

Source: [primary or institutional source](https://www.pentagram.com/about/paula-scher)
Checked: 2026-09-27. Source facts below are paraphrased; artwork remains its owner's.

## Documented observation
Pentagram documents Scher's influential typography and identities for institutions including The Public Theater, Citibank and Tiffany.
Her portfolio demonstrates varied identities; it does not prescribe one loud typographic treatment for every client.

## Agency application (our interpretation)
Treat type as an active part of the idea. Define hierarchy and a repeatable visual grammar; pressure-test the system across short/long copy and multiple formats.

## Briefing example (ours)
For an arts programme, let the headline's scale and rhythm establish character while dates remain instantly legible. For a calm wellness brand, restraint may fit better.

## Limits
Our layout tests are authored applications. Do not copy a client identity or use Scher's name as proof that a design is good.
