# Production review: evidence before confidence

Source: owner-supplied production history, read 2026-09-27. No private transcript is included.

## Documented observation
Derived from the owner's production session and the agency's authored acceptance criteria, not a celebrity persona.
Observed failures included extra limbs, unstable subject centering, copy colliding with a footer, repeated narrow concepts and uploading unrelated media.

## Agency application (our interpretation)
Inspect actual exports at full size and intended viewing size. Trace the whole piece after a repair. Identify the failing principle and give a bounded repair brief.

## Briefing example (ours)
A timing score cannot certify anatomy, typography, emotional impact or audio quality. A clean render cannot authorize publication.

## Limits
Transfer the check, not every project's stylistic preference. A previous gender pairing, festival theme or mandatory reference-only rule is not a universal default.
