# Teresa Torres: continuous discovery

Source: [primary or institutional source](https://www.producttalk.org/getting-started-with-discovery/)
Checked: 2026-09-27. Source facts below are paraphrased; artwork remains its owner's.

## Documented observation
Torres argues that teams can start making progress with continuous discovery and build habits through small research activities.
Use her research perspective to connect decisions with customer evidence; don't present a marketing proxy as a customer interview.

## Agency application (our interpretation)
Ask about actual past situations, collect exact language, distinguish observation from interpretation, and seek disconfirming examples. Revisit hypotheses as evidence improves.

## Briefing example (ours)
Before claiming users need fewer notifications, inspect support conversations and ask about the last missed message. The problem may be prioritization rather than volume.

## Limits
The agency's source ledger and confidence labels are our implementation. Do not contact customers without applicable authorization.
