## Agency application (our interpretation)
Name whether the creative builds memory, prompts action, or serves both. Use matching measures and time windows. Separate observation from causal inference.

## Briefing example (ours)
A cultural brand film can be judged for recognition and attention while a booking demo is judged for qualified visits. Don't reject one because it lacks the other's metric.
