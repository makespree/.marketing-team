# April Dunford: contextual positioning

Source: [primary or institutional source](https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/the-no-differentiation-illusion)
Checked: 2026-09-27. Source facts below are paraphrased; artwork remains its owner's.

## Documented observation
Dunford describes starting with the status quo and shortlisted alternatives, identifying differentiated capabilities and mapping them to customer value.
The method helps reveal why a particular customer cares about a difference. It does not make every product superior to every alternative.

## Agency application (our interpretation)
Compare current alternatives before writing the promise. Separate feature, customer value, best-fit segment and proof. Mark unsupported positioning as a hypothesis.

## Briefing example (ours)
A notes app may matter to offline field researchers because it works without connectivity; don't position it as the best collaboration suite if collaboration is absent.
