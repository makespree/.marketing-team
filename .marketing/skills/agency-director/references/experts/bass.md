# Saul and Elaine Bass: visual storytelling in motion

Sources: [Academy collection](https://www.oscars.org/film-archive/collections/saul-bass-collection),
[Art of the Title's credited work and profile](https://www.artofthetitle.com/designer/elaine-bass/).
Checked: 2026-09-27. Source facts below are paraphrased; artwork remains its owner's.

## Documented observation
The Academy describes Saul Bass's influential film title sequences. Art of the Title documents Elaine's collaboration and reproduces their joint Casino title credit.
Titles can establish a film's visual and emotional world. This short collection record is not a complete account of their creative process.

## Agency application (our interpretation)
Develop a visual motif with a narrative job. Change scale, rhythm and relation to sound to guide attention. Cut unnecessary movement.

## Briefing example (ours)
For an explanation of a network, let one connecting line become the organizing motif across scenes rather than adding an unrelated effect at every cut.

## Limits
These choreography questions are our interpretation. Credit Elaine where the actual work was collaborative; verify individual project credits before attribution.
