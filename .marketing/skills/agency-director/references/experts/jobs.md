# Steve Jobs: product framing

Source: [primary or institutional source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/01/09Apple-Reinvents-the-Phone-with-iPhone/)
Checked: 2026-09-27. Source facts below are paraphrased; artwork remains its owner's.

## Documented observation
Apple's 2007 launch framed iPhone as three familiar product capabilities combined, then described concrete interactions.
Study how a complex product can become one understandable proposition. This source establishes the framing and features; it does not establish every stage direction or presentation habit.

## Agency application (our interpretation)
Ask for the one changed user experience, the contrast with today's alternative, and the live proof. Sequence tension, reveal, demonstration and next action.

## Briefing example (ours)
For a booking app, show the exchange a tutor no longer has to manage, then demonstrate the working booking. Don't copy Apple's stage, phrases or prestige.

## Limits
This is our application of public launch evidence, not an instruction to speak as Jobs. A hardware keynote is not automatically the right model for a local service.
