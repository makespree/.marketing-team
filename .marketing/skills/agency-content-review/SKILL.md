# Content review

Identify the exact revision and files. Read the brief and relevant brand decisions
before judging. Use a fresh reviewer when available; otherwise call it a self-review.
A role name or expert lens does not itself make a review independent.

## Review in this order
1. Meaning: Does the audience understand the intended message? Does the content earn
   attention through the chosen mechanism? Is brand intensity appropriate?
2. Evidence: Verify real claims, named people/places, offer terms and product behaviour.
   Separate sourced fact, owner claim, tradition and inference. No invented proof.
3. Visual: Inspect every slide/frame checkpoint at full size and intended display size.
   Look for anatomy, alpha artefacts, inconsistent identity/anchors, clipping, contrast,
   copied marks, misspelling, bad crops and collisions. Check the whole set after fixes.
4. Motion/audio: Play the encoded file; listen when a playback tool is available.
   Check continuity, readable holds, sound placement and ending. Record if unheard.
   Timing agreement alone does not establish reference fidelity or creative quality.
5. Delivery: Confirm dimensions/duration/codec and current destination constraints,
   ordered files, caption, alt text, credits, provenance and exact hashes.

For each defect: location or timestamp, observed evidence, failed criterion,
priority and bounded repair. Distinguish blocking defects from optional polish.
Use the Director's references/templates/review.md or the job's existing review format.
Never claim to have watched/listened to an artifact from metadata or a contact sheet.

## Handoff
Return ready-for-owner-review, revise, or blocked, with evidence and unperformed checks.
Only record owner approval when given for the exact content; source edits require
a new revision. Review submission, approval, schedule and publication are distinct.
Use scripts/agency.py manifest to hash an explicitly selected set of local files.
It cannot approve, upload or publish. Keep source drafts out of the final upload list.
A destination adapter must verify the stored media/card and save a receipt.
On ambiguous success, reconcile before retrying. Don't back up the whole vault
as an upload side effect. Existing publication receipts survive a revision.
